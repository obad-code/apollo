# What makes a Short worth watching (from the research, Oct 2026)

- Hook in the first 2 seconds: 50-60% of the people who leave, leave in the first 3 seconds. Open on the most striking frame, with movement and a bold text hook on frame one. No intro, no logo.
- A beat every 1.5-3 seconds: a cut, a zoom, a caption pop, a sound. Apollo does this with camera moves, caption pops, transitions and sound accents.
- Captions are not optional (most Shorts are watched muted at first): a few words at a time, big, and inside the safe area - below the top bar, well above the bottom 20% (channel name, description) and clear of the right-hand buttons.
- Sound design beats music: a whoosh on the cut, a thump on the emphasis, clean voice. (Apollo uses no music, by choice.)
- Length: most strong Shorts land around 25-40 seconds; completion is best under ~25. Retention above 70% is the aim; swipe-away under 20% in the first 3 seconds means the hook works.
- Loop it: end on a line that leads back into the first one. A replay counts as another view.
- Structure: hook -> setup -> value -> payoff -> one line worth repeating -> (soft) call to action.
- Voice: ElevenLabs sounds most human; Gemini is good on short punchy lines; Edge is the free fallback.
